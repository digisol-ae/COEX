# CRM contracts and templates

Owner: John. Project created 26 October 2026.

This project covers Worktello's contract categories, reusable templates and customer agreement drafting. We will build it in small, reviewable steps. Product-specific wording will follow the brochures, product details and live project examples that John will provide.

The initial contract drafting feature was implemented and verified locally on 25 October. Its four starters are IT Basic, IT Standard, IT Comprehensive and R4 Annual Maintenance and Support Subscription. The new category structure and additional product templates are planned, not implemented.
