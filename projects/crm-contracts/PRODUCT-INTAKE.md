## Record for each intake

- Product/service name and catalogue identity; parent product and modules where applicable.
- Source filenames, version/date, document owner and relevant clause/page references.
- Included modules, optional modules and excluded functionality.
- Licence/usage metric, quantities, locations, hosting, dependencies and third-party services.
- Contract duration, billing period, charges, setup/training, taxes and agreed payment schedule.
- Implementation/integration scope, deliverables, dependencies, milestones and acceptance.
- Support scope, hours and response commitments actually agreed.
- Data/security/export, renewal, termination, IP and other contract terms supported by approved material.
- Contradictions, unanswered questions, proposed decisions and owner review outcome.

Keep source facts, proposed wording and approved decisions distinct. If brochures and live contracts disagree, record the conflict rather than choosing silently. Remove customer-specific identities and negotiated amounts when creating reusable templates.
