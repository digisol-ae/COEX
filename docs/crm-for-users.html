House rules

Who sees what

Salespeople see their own leads and deals. Managers and administrators see all of them.

Nothing is deleted

Leads are disqualified and deals marked lost, always with a reason. You can always find them again.

Money stays honest

Amounts keep their own currency. AED, USD and PKR are never added together.

Quotes and invoices

These stay in Zoho Books. COEX keeps the quote number as a reference.

Won means contract

Winning a deal offers a pre-filled contract. A manager activates it.

Not included

Quote builder, email campaigns, lead scoring and sales forecasts. They can come later if you want them.

Six answers we need from you

  1. Who sells: a few named people, or everyone on the team?
  2. Is one pipeline enough, or do new clinics and upgrades need different stages?
  3. Are the stages right (Qualified, Needs analysis, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Won, Lost)?
  4. What reasons do you hear when a deal is lost (price, competitor, timing, no budget, no reply)?
  5. Should a deal show one-off money (licence, setup) apart from recurring money (AMC)?
  6. Where do your leads come from today? This becomes the Source list.
